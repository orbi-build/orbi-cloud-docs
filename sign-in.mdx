Signing in to Orbi Cloud is a single GitHub OAuth click. The process requests only your identity and email address — Orbi cannot read, write, or interact with any of your repositories until you separately install the GitHub App.

## Sign In

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Orbi status page">
    Navigate to [orbi.build/api/](https://orbi.build/api/) in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Sign in with GitHub">
    Click the **Sign in with GitHub** button on the landing screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the OAuth request">
    GitHub will ask you to approve the `user:email` scope. This grants Orbi access to your GitHub identity and email address only. Click **Authorize** to continue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Signing In Grants

Signing in with GitHub OAuth establishes your identity inside Orbi Cloud. It does **not** grant Orbi access to your repositories, issues, pull requests, or any code.

<Info>
  If you ever want to review what OAuth scopes Orbi holds, go to **GitHub → Settings → Applications → Authorized OAuth Apps**.
</Info>

## Personal and Organization Accounts

Each GitHub account — whether a personal account or an organization — creates its own independent Orbi tenant. Tenants are fully isolated from one another. Each has its own:

- Repository connection
- Subscription and billing
- Delivery history and usage
- Model configuration

If you sign in with a personal account and later need to work under an organization, sign out and sign back in with the organization account. The two tenants will not share any data.

## Language

Orbi Cloud supports **English** and **Chinese**. Toggle between them using the 🇬🇧 and 🇨🇳 flag buttons in the top-right corner of the status page. Your choice is stored in a browser cookie and persists for **1 year**, so the page will remember your preferred language across visits.

## Sessions

A session lasts **7 days** from the moment you sign in. Sessions are **not** extended by activity — browsing the status page or triggering deliveries does not reset the clock.

When your session expires, the status page displays:

<Frame caption="The signed-out confirmation page">
  <img src="/images/signed-out.png" alt="The signed-out confirmation page" />
</Frame>

Click the **Sign in again** link to start a new session. All your tenant data — repository connection, delivery history, and model configuration — is preserved between sessions. Nothing is lost when a session expires.

## Signing Out

To sign out, scroll to the **Account** card at the bottom of the status page and click **Sign out**.

<Note>
  Signing out clears your session on that device only. It does not stop a delivery that is already in progress, and it does not cancel your subscription. Return and sign in again at any time to resume where you left off.
</Note>
