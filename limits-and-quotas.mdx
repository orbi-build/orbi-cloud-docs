Three limits apply: a free allowance of 3 merged deliveries before you subscribe, a monthly token allowance that depends on your plan, and a limit on how many repositories can be connected at once.

None of them ever interrupts a delivery that is already running.

## At a glance

| | Free | Solo | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deliveries | First 3 merged deliveries | Unlimited within the token allowance | Unlimited within the token allowance |
| Model tokens per month | Not a practical limit at 3 deliveries | 100,000,000 | 300,000,000 |
| Repositories connected at once | 1 | 1 | 5 |

Prices and the yearly option are on the [pricing page](https://orbi.build/cloud/?ref=cloud-docs-limits); [billing](/billing) covers subscribing.

## The free allowance

Your first **3 merged deliveries are free**. No card, no subscription.

A slot is taken when you label an Issue `ai-ready`, and it is **refunded** if that delivery does not end in a merge — an Issue that ends `ai-blocked`, or that you close without merging, gives its slot back. That is why the allowance is described as three *merged* deliveries rather than three attempts.

Counting is per Issue and idempotent: re-labelling the same Issue does not consume another slot. Re-delivering the same Issue after a refund uses the slot it gave back.

Orbi keeps you informed as you go: the merge comment on a free delivery says how many free deliveries remain, and the status page shows **Your first 3 deliveries are free (N left)** in the **Next** card.

### When the allowance runs out

The next Issue you label `ai-ready` has that label removed and gets a comment saying the free deliveries are used up, with a link to subscribe. The status page says **Your 3 free deliveries are used up** with a **Subscribe** button, and the last setup step reads **Subscribe after your 3 free deliveries**.

**To resume:** subscribe, then **re-apply the `ai-ready` label** to the Issue. The label is not restored automatically. Deliveries already in flight when the allowance ran out finish normally.

## The monthly token allowance

A subscription includes model usage — **100,000,000 tokens a month on Solo, 300,000,000 on Pro** — counted per calendar month in UTC. There is no per-token overage billing.

Usage counts input tokens, output tokens and cache-read tokens. Re-delivering the same Issue accumulates: each delivery is a separate run.

How many deliveries that buys depends on how large the tasks and the codebase are, and on prompt caching. The [pricing page](https://orbi.build/cloud/?ref=cloud-docs-limits) gives current measured ranges.

### Watching your usage

Open **Settings and reference** on the status page. The **Monthly usage** line shows tokens used against your allowance, the percentage used and the **Cache hit rate**, with a bar that turns amber at 80% and red at 100%.

The cache hit rate matters more than it looks. Cache-miss tokens cost roughly **50 times** more than cache hits, so a low rate burns the allowance noticeably faster. If yours is low, it is worth understanding why before assuming the allowance is too small.

Each work item in **Delivery history** also shows its own usage — runtime, model requests, input and output tokens, and cache use. Work that never reported usage shows `—`.

### When the monthly quota runs out

Orbi **pauses claiming new Issues**. Your `ai-ready` labels stay where they are, and deliveries already running finish normally.

The **Next** card says **Monthly quota exhausted: Orbi has paused claiming new Issues; in-progress deliveries will finish normally**, and offers the ways out:

- **Connect your own model key.** Saving a key in **Model config** resumes delivery immediately, with no quota limit — see [model configuration](/model-configuration).
- **Upgrade to Pro** (on Solo), for 300,000,000 tokens a month.
- **Or wait.** On the 1st of the next month (UTC) the allowance resets and the waiting Issues start automatically. You do not need to re-apply any label.

## The repository limit

Free and Solo connect **one repository at a time**; Pro connects **up to five**. The rule is the same on every plan: at the limit, connecting another repository is refused with **Repository limit reached**, so deactivate one first, then connect the new one. Free and Solo can also upgrade to Pro. See [connect a repository](/connect-repository#changing-the-connected-repository).

## What is never interrupted

No limit ever stops work in progress. A delivery that is `ai-in-progress`, `ai-pr-opened` or `ai-fix-needed` runs to completion regardless.

Platform upgrades and deployments have the same property: they never restart a delivery that is already executing.

## Summary

| Limit | Value | At the limit | To resume |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free allowance | 3 merged deliveries | `ai-ready` removed, comment with subscribe link | Subscribe, re-apply `ai-ready` |
| Monthly tokens | Solo 100,000,000 · Pro 300,000,000, per calendar month (UTC) | New Issues wait; labels stay | Automatic on the 1st (UTC), or immediately with your own key or an upgrade |
| Repositories | Free and Solo 1 · Pro 5 | Connecting another is refused | Deactivate one first, or upgrade |
| Pickup interval | Up to 5 minutes | — | — |
