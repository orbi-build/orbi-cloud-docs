Two limits apply to your Orbi Cloud account: the **free allowance** of 3 merged deliveries before you need a subscription, and the **monthly token quota** of 300M tokens per calendar month included with your subscription. Both limits work the same way — they only act on the moment a new Issue is dispatched. Deliveries already in flight are never interrupted.

## The Free Allowance

Your account starts with 3 merged deliveries that require no payment or card on file.

- **A slot is reserved** when you apply the `ai-ready` label to an Issue.
- **Refunded automatically** if the delivery doesn't end in a merge — that is, if it is blocked, closed, or cancelled without a PR merging.
- **Counted per Issue and idempotent** — re-labelling the same Issue after a refund does not consume a new slot; only distinct merged outcomes count.

### When the Allowance Runs Out

When you dispatch an Issue and no free slots remain:

1. Orbi removes the `ai-ready` label automatically.
2. A comment is posted on the Issue explaining that the free allowance is exhausted and providing a link to subscribe.
3. Any deliveries already in progress finish normally — they are not affected.

To resume, subscribe through the Account card on the status page, then re-apply the `ai-ready` label to your Issue manually.

## The Monthly Token Quota

**What counts toward your quota:**

- Input tokens sent to the model
- Output tokens generated by the model
- Cache-read tokens (charged at a lower rate by most providers, but still counted)

Each delivery is measured independently. Re-delivering the same Issue accumulates usage — every run is a separate billing event.

**Rough capacity guide:** depending on task size and how well prompt caching performs, 300M tokens supports approximately 85–400 merged deliveries per month.

### Watching Your Usage

The status page gives you several ways to track consumption:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Monthly Usage" icon="chart-bar">
    Shows total tokens used, tokens remaining, and a percentage of your monthly cap. Updated after each delivery completes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Monthly Cache Hit Rate" icon="bolt">
    Cache-miss tokens cost roughly 50× more than cache hits. A rate below 90% burns through your quota significantly faster.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Each individual work item also shows a detailed breakdown: input tokens, output tokens, cache tokens, wall-clock runtime, number of model requests, and how many times the delivery was resumed.

### When the Quota Runs Out

When you dispatch an Issue and the monthly quota is exhausted:

1. Orbi removes the `ai-ready` label.
2. A comment is posted on the Issue naming your current usage and the 300M cap.
3. In-flight deliveries are not affected.

To resume before the next calendar month, [configure BYOK](/model-configuration) — BYOK usage draws from your own provider account and is never paused by the platform quota. You must re-apply `ai-ready` yourself in either case.

<Note>
  If the platform's usage measurement system is temporarily unhealthy, Orbi fails closed — new dispatch pauses rather than spending tokens against numbers that can't be verified. This condition clears automatically once measurement is restored; no action is needed on your part.
</Note>

## What Is Never Interrupted

Both guards — the free allowance check and the token quota check — fire only at the instant an Issue is labelled `ai-ready` and has no other `ai-*` label. Once a delivery is running, neither limit can stop it.

Platform upgrades follow the same rule: Orbi never interrupts a delivery that is already in progress, regardless of what is being deployed.

## The One-Repository Limit

Orbi Cloud connects one repository at a time. You can change your connected repository at any time from the status page, with one exception: you cannot switch repositories while a delivery is in flight. Wait for the active delivery to complete (or for it to reach a terminal label), then reconnect.

For step-by-step instructions, see [Connect a Repository — Changing Your Repository](/connect-repository#changing).
