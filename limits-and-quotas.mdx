Three limits apply: a free allowance of 3 merged deliveries before you subscribe, a monthly token allowance that depends on your paid plan, and a limit on how many repositories can be connected at once.

None of them ever interrupts a delivery that is already running.

## At a glance

| | Free | Solo | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deliveries | First 3 merged deliveries | Unlimited within the token allowance | Unlimited within the token allowance |
| Model tokens per month | No allowance shown; the free deliveries are the limit | 400,000,000 (400M) | 1,200,000,000 (1.2B) |
| Repositories connected at once | 1 | 1 | 5 |

Prices and the yearly option are on the [pricing page](https://orbi.build/cloud/?ref=cloud-docs-limits); [billing](/billing) covers subscribing.

## The free allowance

Your first 3 merged deliveries are free. No card, no subscription.

A slot is taken when you label an Issue `ai-ready`, and it is refunded if that delivery does not end in a merge: an Issue that ends `ai-blocked`, or that you close without merging, gives its slot back. That is why the allowance is described as three *merged* deliveries rather than three attempts. The count does not reset monthly.

Counting is per Issue and idempotent: re-labelling the same Issue does not consume another slot. Re-delivering the same Issue after a refund takes a slot again.

Orbi keeps you informed as you go. The merge comment on a free delivery says how many free deliveries remain. On the status page, the **Next** card shows **Free: N left (of 3).**, and the last step of **Setup progress** reads **First 3 free**; hovering over it explains how a task is counted.

### When the allowance runs out

The next Issue you label `ai-ready` has that label removed and gets a comment saying the free deliveries are used up, with a link to subscribe. The status page says **Free 3 used up:** **subscribe to keep delivering →** with a **Subscribe** button.

To resume, subscribe, then re-apply the `ai-ready` label to the Issue. The label is not restored automatically. Deliveries already in flight when the allowance ran out finish normally.

## The monthly token allowance

A paid subscription includes model usage: 400,000,000 tokens a month on Solo, 1,200,000,000 on Pro, counted per calendar month in UTC. The status page shows the reset as the 1st at 8:00 Beijing time, which is the same moment as 00:00 UTC. There is no per-token overage billing. Free accounts see no monthly allowance; their limit is the free deliveries.

Usage counts input tokens, output tokens and cache-read tokens, all at the same weight. Re-delivering the same Issue accumulates: each delivery is a separate run.

How many deliveries that buys depends on how large the tasks and the codebase are, and on prompt caching. The [pricing page](https://orbi.build/cloud/?ref=cloud-docs-limits) gives current measured ranges.

### Watching your usage

Open **Usage and settings** on the status page. On a paid plan the **Monthly AI usage** line shows tokens used against your allowance, the **Used** percentage, the **Cached** share and when it resets, with a bar that turns amber at 80% and red at 100%. On the free plan the line shows only the tokens used, with no cap, percentage or bar.

**Cached** is the share of this month's tokens that were cache reads. Cache-read tokens count against the allowance just like input and output tokens. If some tasks did not report their usage, the line says how many were left out of the total.

### When the monthly quota runs out

Orbi pauses claiming new Issues. Your `ai-ready` labels stay where they are, and deliveries already running finish normally.

The **Next** card says **Monthly AI usage is used up: Orbi has paused new tasks; in-progress ones will finish normally.** and offers the ways out:

- **Use your own API key**. Saving a key in the model configuration resumes delivery immediately, does not count against this month, and is billed by your AI model provider. See [model configuration](/model-configuration).
- **Upgrade to Pro** (shown on Solo), for 1,200,000,000 tokens a month.
- **Or wait**. On the 1st of the next month at 8:00 Beijing time (00:00 UTC) the allowance resets and the waiting Issues start automatically. You do not need to re-apply any label.

## The repository limit

Free and Solo connect one repository at a time; Pro connects up to five. The rule is the same on every plan: at the limit, connecting another repository is refused with **Repository limit reached**, so disconnect one first with **Disconnect a repository**, then connect the new one. Disconnecting does not change the repository on GitHub. Free and Solo can also upgrade to Pro. See [connect a repository](/connect-repository#changing-the-connected-repository).

## What is never interrupted

No limit ever stops work in progress. A delivery that is `ai-in-progress`, `ai-pr-opened` or `ai-fix-needed` runs to completion regardless.

Platform upgrades and deployments have the same property: they never restart a delivery that is already executing.

## Summary

| Limit | Value | At the limit | To resume |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free allowance | 3 merged deliveries | `ai-ready` removed, comment with subscribe link | Subscribe, re-apply `ai-ready` |
| Monthly tokens | Solo 400,000,000 · Pro 1,200,000,000, per calendar month (UTC) | New Issues wait; labels stay | Automatic on the 1st (00:00 UTC, 8:00 Beijing time), or immediately with your own key or an upgrade |
| Repositories | Free and Solo 1 · Pro 5 | Connecting another is refused | Disconnect one first, or upgrade |
| Pickup wait | Usually under a minute, about 5 minutes at most | — | — |
