The status page at **[orbi.build/api/](https://orbi.build/api/)** is where you see what Orbi is doing, what needs you, and what you have used.

It is ordered by what you need to do, not by feature. From top to bottom:

1. The **top bar** — **Orbi** on the left (it always brings you back here), your avatar and the account menu on the right.
2. **Next** — what is happening and the one thing you should do now.
3. The **repository overview** — one row per connected repository.
4. **Delivery history** — every piece of work, in three tabs.
5. **Settings and reference** — usage, repositories and versions, folded away until you open it.
6. The **footer** — support, service status, privacy, terms and GitHub.

## Next

The **Next** card is the part of the page to read first. It holds, in order:

- **Setup progress** — the seven onboarding steps (see below). Once all seven are done it folds into **Setup complete · 7/7**.
- A **headline** — a one-line summary of your account's state.
- **① Needs you** or **① Action reminders** — the items waiting on you, each with the button that deals with it. When nothing needs you, this part is absent; that is good news.
- **Pick one for Orbi to try** — when Orbi is idle, a starter Issue and your unlabelled Issues, each with **Try →**. See [first Issue](/first-issue#dispatching-from-the-status-page).
- The page's one solid button, when there is an obvious next action — for example **Create Issue** when Orbi has finished and is waiting for more work.

### Headlines

| Headline | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Connect a repository first** | No repository is connected yet |
| **Orbi is preparing the environment** | [Provisioning](/provisioning) is running; usually one to two minutes |
| **Provisioning failed; the platform will retry automatically** | Provisioning failed for a transient reason; Orbi retries on its own |
| **Provisioning failed; the platform is recovering automatically** | A platform-side failure; no action needed — see [provisioning](/provisioning#when-provisioning-fails) |
| **Orbi is waiting for your first Issue** | Ready. Label an Issue `ai-ready`, or use **Try →** |
| **Orbi is coding** | A delivery is in progress |
| **Orbi is reviewing PR #N and will merge it when it passes** | The independent review is running |
| **PR #N needs your approval** | Branch protection requires an approval before merge — see [branch protection](/connect-repository#branch-protection-that-blocks-every-merge) |
| **Orbi needs your confirmation** | A result is waiting for a human to confirm it |
| **Orbi is stuck and needs you** | An Issue is blocked, or something on your side needs fixing; the item under **① Needs you** says which |
| **Orbi is standing by** | Nothing is running; an Issue may be waiting outside the current delivery scope, or need your attention |
| **Orbi is idle** | Everything is finished; Orbi is waiting for the next Issue |
| **Can't reach Orbi right now** | Orbi could not read your delivery state at the moment; the card offers a refresh or a reconnect |

Two situations replace the headline's usual button with their own way out:

- **Your 3 free deliveries are used up** — with a **Subscribe** button. See [billing](/billing).
- **Monthly quota exhausted** — Orbi pauses claiming new Issues, while deliveries already running finish normally. The card offers **Connect your own model key**, and on Solo also an upgrade to Pro. See [limits and quotas](/limits-and-quotas#when-the-monthly-quota-runs-out).

### Setup progress

Seven steps track onboarding: **Sign in → Install App → Connect repository → Provision environment → First Issue → Cut a release → Subscribe**. Each step is marked **✓ Done**, **Waiting for you**, **System is working** or **Failed**; the current one is highlighted and has a **How to do this step ▶** link that plays a short video of that step. For accounts still on the free deliveries, the last step reads **Subscribe after your 3 free deliveries**, with a folded **Pricing and account** note.

## Repository overview

A table headed **Repository N / limit M**, with an **Add repository** button, and one row per connected repository: how many of its Issues are **In progress**, **Under review** and **Done**. A row that needs attention carries a chip — **Needs you**, **Retrying automatically** or **Platform recovering**.

With more than one repository, click a row to open that repository's own view: its base branch and current delivery scope, **View on GitHub** and **Create Issue**, what is happening in it now, its delivery history, its versions, and **Deactivate this repository**. See [more than one repository](/connect-repository#more-than-one-repository).

## Delivery history

Your work, split across three tabs. Each Issue appears in exactly one of them:

| Tab | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **In progress** | Open work that has no pull request yet — queued, being coded, waiting for detail, or blocked before a pull request was opened |
| **Under review** | Open work that has a pull request — going through the independent review, being repaired, waiting for your approval, or blocked after review |
| **Done** | Finished work: merged, or closed |

Each row shows the Issue (linking to GitHub), a status chip, its pull request and when it was last updated. Under the title you see its usage once the delivery reports it — runtime, model requests, input and output tokens, cache use — and, for queued work, **Queued** or **Waiting for a prerequisite Issue**.

The chips map to the labels described in [the delivery lifecycle](/delivery-lifecycle#what-each-label-means): **Queued**, **In progress**, **PR under review**, **Fix needed**, **Awaiting approval**, **Needs detail**, **Needs your confirmation**, **Blocked**, **Merged**, **Closed**. Open the Issue to see the reason and the next step in Orbi's comment.

## Settings and reference

Folded by default — click **Settings and reference** to open it.

<Frame caption="Settings and reference, opened: monthly usage, the connected repository and versions">
  <img src="/images/status-settings.png" alt="Settings and reference, opened: monthly usage, the connected repository and versions" />
</Frame>

**Monthly usage.** One line: tokens used this calendar month against your plan's allowance, the percentage used, and the **Cache hit rate**, with a bar that turns amber at 80% and red at 100%. With your own model key it reads **Monthly usage (your key)** and has no limit. A low cache hit rate is worth noticing: cache-miss tokens cost about 50 times more than cache hits, so they use up the allowance much faster. See [limits and quotas](/limits-and-quotas).

**Connected repository.** Each repository with its **Base branch** and **Provisioning status** — and, when provisioning failed, the reason, the time, a **Request ID** and the next step. Repositories you disconnected are listed under **Historical repositories** with their history intact.

**Versions.** The current delivery scope, your unreleased versions with **Start delivery** and **Release** actions, and a **New version** box. See [releases](/releases).

For accounts on the free deliveries, a **Founding-member offer** note can also appear here after the first merged delivery.

## The account menu and the footer

Subscription, **Model config**, language, support, these docs and **Sign out** are all in the account menu under your avatar — see [the account menu](/sign-in#the-account-menu).

The footer on every page links to **Support (Telegram)**, **Status** (the service status page), **Privacy**, **Terms** and **GitHub**.

## Refreshing

The page refreshes itself while provisioning is running, so you can leave it open during that minute or two. Otherwise reload it to get the current state — it reads GitHub live, so what it shows is the real state of your Issues, not a cached copy.

If Orbi cannot reach GitHub at the moment, the page says **Can't reach Orbi right now** or that delivery data is temporarily unavailable, rather than showing you a misleading empty list.
