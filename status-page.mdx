The status page lives at [orbi.build/api/](https://orbi.build/api/) and is your single view into everything Orbi is doing on your behalf. It is organised by urgency — things that need your attention come first, active work comes second, and reference information sits at the bottom. You do not need to dig through settings to find out whether something is wrong.

## Page Layout

The page is divided into three numbered sections. You can think of them as three questions answered in order.

## ① Needs You

This section appears only when something requires your action. The banner names the problem in plain language and gives you the button or link to resolve it. Once you act, the banner disappears.

When nothing needs your attention, this section is absent entirely. An empty ① is good news.

## ② Happening Now

The headline sentence at the top of this section gives you a plain-language summary of Orbi's current state — for example, "Orbi is coding", "Orbi is idle", or "Orbi is stuck and needs you". Below it, three tabs break down the work in detail.

| Tab | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Doing** | Work Orbi is currently handling — any Issue carrying an active `ai-*` label |
| **Awaiting** | Pull requests waiting for your review or approval |
| **Done** | Finished work: Issues that reached `ai-merged` or were closed |

Each row in the table shows a link to the work item, a status chip, a PR link where applicable, the time of the last update, and usage for that item. Status chips map directly to labels: **In Progress** corresponds to `ai-in-progress`, **Fix Needed** to `ai-fix-needed`, **Blocked** to `ai-blocked`, and so on.

### Setup Progress

If you have not yet completed the initial setup, a 7-step breadcrumb appears in this section:

**Sign in → Install App → Connect repository → Provision environment → First Issue → Cut a release → Subscribe**

It is the fastest way to see how far you got.

## ③ Reference

This section contains five cards. They do not change frequently, but they hold the information you need when something goes wrong or when you want to adjust your configuration.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Monthly Usage">
    Shows the number of tokens used against your monthly limit, displayed as a percentage bar. The cache hit rate appears beside it. A cache hit rate below 90% means most requests are missing the cache and burning quota roughly 50 times faster than cached requests. If you see a low hit rate, check whether your model or key configuration has changed recently.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Connected Repository">
    Shows your active repository, the base branch Orbi merges into, and the current provisioning status. This card is also where you connect a new repository, switch to a different one, and access the **Cut a release** form.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Model Configuration">
    Shows whether Orbi is using your platform quota or a bring-your-own-key (BYOK) configuration. If you have configured a BYOK API key, it appears masked here. Use this card to add, update, or remove a key and to select which of the 20+ supported providers to use.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Account">
    Shows your GitHub login and your current subscription status. The **Subscribe** or **Manage subscription** button is here, as is the **Sign out** link.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The fifth card, **Technical Support**, contains a link to the Telegram support group and a QR code you can scan on mobile. Response from the Orbi team comes through that group.

## Subscription Status Values

The Account card displays one of the following status values.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Founding Pilot | Active paid subscription at the founding rate |
| Subscription active | Subscription is in good standing |
| Past due (payment failed) | Stripe could not charge your payment method — update your card to restore access |

## Page Refresh

During repository provisioning, the page auto-refreshes so you can watch the steps complete without reloading. At all other times, reload manually to see the latest state. The page reads GitHub live on every load — there is no cached copy of your work items. If GitHub is unreachable, the page shows "Can't reach Orbi right now" rather than an empty list that could be mistaken for no active work.

## Mobile

Whether you are on the included platform quota or your own key. If you configured a key, it is shown masked. See [model configuration](/model-configuration).

### Account

Your GitHub login, your subscription status, and the buttons for **Manage subscription** (or **Subscribe with Stripe**) and **Sign out**.

### Technical support

A link and a QR code for the Telegram group. That is the support channel — see [troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#getting-help).

## Refreshing

The page refreshes itself while provisioning is running, so you can leave it open during that minute or two. Otherwise reload it to get the current state — it reads GitHub live, so what it shows is the real state of your Issues, not a cached copy.

If Orbi cannot reach GitHub at the moment, the page says **Can't reach Orbi right now** or that delivery data is temporarily unavailable, rather than showing you a misleading empty list.
