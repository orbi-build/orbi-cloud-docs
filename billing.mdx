All subscription management for Orbi Cloud runs through Stripe, which you reach from the **Account** card on the status page at [orbi.build/api/](https://orbi.build/api/). Orbi Cloud never stores your card number or payment details — those live entirely with Stripe.

## The Plan

| | |
|---|---|
| **Free allowance** | 3 merged deliveries, no card required |
| **Price** | US$79 per month |
| **Included model usage** | 300,000,000 tokens per calendar month |
| **Overage billing** | None — deliveries pause at the cap instead |

<Note>
  Failed deliveries do not consume your free allowance. See [Limits & Quotas](/limits-and-quotas) for the exact rules on how merged deliveries are counted.
</Note>

## Subscribe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Account card">
    On the status page, find the **Account** card and click **Subscribe with Stripe**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment on Stripe Checkout">
    Enter your payment details on the Stripe-hosted checkout page. Orbi Cloud does not see or store your card information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Orbi Cloud">
    After payment, Stripe redirects you back to the status page. Your subscription activates immediately — no waiting period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You don't need to subscribe before trying Orbi Cloud. Sign-in, GitHub App installation, repository connection, provisioning, and your first 3 merged deliveries are all completely free. Subscribe only when you're ready to go further.
</Note>

## Manage Your Subscription

After subscribing, the **Subscribe with Stripe** button becomes **Manage subscription**. Clicking it opens the Stripe billing portal, where you can:

- Update your payment method
- Download past invoices
- Cancel your subscription

Any changes you make in the portal take effect immediately and are reflected in the Account card on your next page load.

## Subscription Status

The Account card on the status page shows your current subscription status. The table below explains each status value.

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Founding Pilot** | An active paid subscription at the founding partner rate |
| **Subscription active** | Subscription in good standing at the standard rate |
| **Past due (payment failed)** | Stripe was unable to charge your payment method |

<Warning>
  If your subscription is past due, fix your payment method in the Stripe billing portal as soon as possible. While past due, new Issues are handled under unsubscribed (free allowance) rules — the monthly token quota and dispatch behaviour revert to the free tier.
</Warning>

## Cancelling

Cancel your subscription at any time through the Stripe billing portal via **Manage subscription**. Cancelling:

- **Stops future charges** immediately — you will not be billed again.
- **Does not delete** your repository connection, delivery history, or model configuration.
- **Preserves your free allowance** — whatever remains of your 3 merged deliveries carries over.

<Note>
  Cancelling a subscription and uninstalling the GitHub App are two separate actions. Cancel through Stripe to stop billing. Uninstall the Orbi GitHub App from your GitHub settings to remove Orbi's access to your repository code. You can do one without the other.
</Note>

## Invoices

All invoices are available in the Stripe billing portal under **Manage subscription**. Orbi Cloud does not email invoices separately — go to the portal whenever you need a receipt or statement for your records.

## Founding Partner

The first 10 subscribers to Orbi Cloud are **Founding Partners**. The Founding Partner programme offers:

- Subscription is free, with 300M monthly tokens covered by Orbi
- In exchange, you commit to one accepted Issue per month that helps steer product direction

Founding Partner coupons are limited. Once all 10 have been claimed, new subscriptions are at the standard US$79/month rate.

<Note>
  **Founding Pilot** and **Founding Partner** are two different things. "Founding Pilot" is the plan name shown in your Account status line — it means you have an active subscription at the founding rate. "Founding Partner" refers to the programme for the first 10 subscribers. If your status shows "Founding Pilot", you are a Founding Partner.
</Note>
