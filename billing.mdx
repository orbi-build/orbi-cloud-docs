You can try Orbi Cloud without paying anything, then subscribe to Solo or Pro through Stripe from the account menu.

Orbi Cloud does not hold your card details. Payment, invoices and cancellation are handled by Stripe's own hosted pages.

## The plans

| | Free | Solo | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| What you get | Your first **3 merged deliveries** | Deliveries within the monthly allowance | Deliveries within the monthly allowance |
| Model usage included | — | **100,000,000 tokens** a month | **300,000,000 tokens** a month |
| Repositories connected at once | 1 | 1 | Up to 5 |
| Card required | No | Yes | Yes |

Both paid plans can be billed monthly or yearly, and neither has per-token overage billing — when the month's allowance runs out, new deliveries pause instead. Current prices, the yearly discount and any founding offer are on the **[pricing page](https://orbi.build/cloud/?ref=cloud-docs-billing)**, which is the reference for what you will be charged.

Failed deliveries never consume the free allowance, and you can use your own model key on any plan. See [limits and quotas](/limits-and-quotas) for exactly how each allowance is counted.

**Which plan?** Solo fits one repository and a steady trickle of Issues. Pick Pro when you want several repositories connected at once, or when Solo's monthly allowance runs out before the month does.

Self-hosting the open-source [Orbi](https://orbi.build/?ref=cloud-docs-billing) engine is free, and stays free.

## Subscribe

There are two ways in:

- **From the pricing page.** Choose Solo or Pro, monthly or yearly, on [orbi.build/cloud](https://orbi.build/cloud/?ref=cloud-docs-billing). If you are not signed in yet, you sign in with GitHub first; your choice is kept, and the account menu shows **You picked … on the pricing page — Continue subscription** until you finish.
- **From the account menu.** Click your avatar, then **Subscribe with Stripe**. This subscribes you to Pro, billed monthly; use the pricing page when you want Solo or yearly billing.

Either way you complete payment on Stripe Checkout, where you can also enter a promotion code, and Stripe returns you to Orbi Cloud.

Your subscription activates as soon as Stripe confirms the payment. The account menu shows **Subscription active**, and your plan's name appears next to your login. You do not need to reconnect your repository or reconfigure anything.

<Note>
You do not have to subscribe before trying Orbi Cloud. Sign-in, App installation, connecting a repository, provisioning and your first three merged deliveries are all free.
</Note>

## Managing your subscription

Once subscribed, the account menu shows **Manage subscription** instead. It opens the Stripe billing portal, where you can update your payment method, download invoices and cancel.

On Solo, when your monthly allowance runs out the status page also offers **Upgrade to Pro** directly — see [limits and quotas](/limits-and-quotas#when-the-monthly-quota-runs-out).

## Subscription states

The chip next to your login in the account menu shows your plan:

| What it says | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Not subscribed** | On the free allowance |
| **Solo** or **Pro** | An active paid subscription on that plan |
| **Founding Pilot** or **Design Partner** | An active subscription from an early-access programme |
| **Past due (payment failed)** | Stripe could not charge your payment method |

If you are **past due**, fix the payment method in the same billing portal — **Manage subscription** stays available in that state precisely so you can repair it. While past due, your account is treated as unsubscribed for dispatch, so new Issues are subject to the free allowance rules.

## Cancelling

Cancel through the Stripe billing portal. Cancelling stops future charges; it does not delete your repository connections, your delivery history or your model configuration.

After cancelling, new dispatch follows the unsubscribed rules described in [limits and quotas](/limits-and-quotas).

To remove Orbi's access to your code entirely, [uninstall the GitHub App](/install-app#revoking). Cancelling a subscription and uninstalling the App are separate actions.

## Invoices and receipts

All invoices live in the Stripe billing portal under **Manage subscription**. Orbi Cloud does not email them separately.

## Questions about a charge

Billing and refund questions go to **support@orbi.build** — see [getting help](/troubleshooting#getting-help).
